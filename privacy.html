Trellis

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 6, 2026

Trellis is a task, calendar, and weather app. This policy explains what information Trellis accesses, why, and where it goes. There is no Trellis server — your tasks, events, and settings are stored only on your device.

Location

If you grant location access, Trellis uses your device's location to fetch a weather forecast and radar imagery for where you are. Your coordinates are sent directly to whichever weather provider you've selected — Open-Meteo, the U.S. National Weather Service, or OpenWeatherMap — to retrieve that forecast. Trellis does not store your location history or share it with anyone else. You can also search for a location by name instead of granting location access.

Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar, Trellis requests permission to view and manage events (the calendar.events scope) so it can show your Google events alongside your tasks and add events you create in Trellis back to your calendar. Trellis never accesses any other part of your Google account.

If you enable on-device calendars (e.g. a Samsung or Outlook calendar already on your phone), Trellis reads those events read-only, to display them next to your tasks. It never edits or deletes on-device calendar events.

Notifications and alarms

Trellis uses notification and exact-alarm permissions to remind you about tasks, deliver your daily summary, and alert you to severe weather. These are scheduled and shown entirely on your device.

What Trellis does not do

Trellis has no user accounts, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and shows no ads. Nothing you enter into Trellis — tasks, events, notes — is uploaded anywhere. The only network requests Trellis makes are the ones described above: fetching weather for a location, and syncing with Google Calendar if you choose to connect it. Trellis does not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Your tasks, events, and settings live in local app storage until you delete them or uninstall Trellis, at which point they're gone. Disconnecting Google Calendar in Settings revokes Trellis's access immediately.

Children's privacy

Trellis is not directed at children under 13, and Trellis does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
