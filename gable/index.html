Gable

Weather and radar with a little personality.

Coming soon to Google Play

Gable is a weather app for people who actually look at the radar. Forecasts come from the National Weather Service, written by local forecasters, and the radar comes straight from NOAA: the same data the pros use, free, with no ads.

It also doesn't take itself too seriously. The sky on the forecast card moves with the weather, and Gable has a few opinions about it.

Radar that's actually free

High-resolution radar from your nearest NOAA station, including velocity, hail and precipitation type, and rainfall totals, plus the national MRMS mosaic. Press and hold anywhere for the exact value.

Forecasts from local forecasters

National Weather Service forecasts in the U.S., with the forecaster's own summary for each day. MET Norway everywhere else.

Severe weather alerts

NWS warnings for your places, on the forecast and as notifications, with quiet hours that never silence a tornado or flash flood warning. Always free.

Rain soon

When rain is starting or stopping in the next six hours, down to 15 minutes, from NOAA's HRRR model.

Charts and day details

Drag across temperature, rain and wind charts, and open any day for UV, sunrise and sunset, the moon, dew point, pressure and more.

Future radar

Where the rain is headed over the next hours, from Gable's own HRRR forecast radar.

Gable Pro

Warnings, alerts and all radar stay free. Pro adds your choice of forecast source (ECMWF, GFS, ICON and more), the full 18-hour Future radar, 48-hour charts, every NOAA map layer, extra palettes and unlimited places. Pro+ adds side-by-side model comparisons.
