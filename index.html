Tasks, calendar and weather

Trellis

A planner with a garden that grows as you get things done.

  • Tasks, a time-blocked schedule and your calendar in one place
  • Every finished day plants a flower in your garden
  • Morning and evening rituals, a journal, and weather with attitude

How Trellis uses Google Calendar: If you connect it, Trellis lists your subscribed calendars and displays their events alongside your tasks. You can create, edit, or delete events on calendars your Google account can edit. Calendar data travels directly between your device and Google over HTTPS; it does not pass through Trellis's server. Optional weather, feedback, purchase, and Drive backup data are described in the privacy policy.

About

Why "Millwright"?

A millwright is the person who builds and maintains the machinery that keeps everything running. That's the idea here: dependable tools, built with care rather than chasing trends.

Millwright is one person. No team, no funding, just someone who likes building useful, well-made things.

  1. Made to be used every day

    Apps you open without thinking, because they're quick, clear and do what they say.

  2. Your data, handled honestly

    Only what each feature needs, explained in plain language in each app's privacy policy.

  3. A little character

    A garden that grows with your to-do list. A weather app with opinions. Useful doesn't have to be dull.
