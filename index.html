Millwright Apps

Solid, dependable tools, built with care.

Millwright is a one-person app studio. No team, no funding — just someone who likes building useful, well-made things and figuring it out as I go.

The name comes from the old trade: a millwright is the person who builds and maintains the machinery that keeps everything running. That's the idea here — dependable tools, built with care rather than chasing trends.
