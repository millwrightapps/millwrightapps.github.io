Solid, dependable tools, built with care.

Millwright is a one-person app studio. No team, no funding — just someone who likes building useful, well-made things and figuring it out as I go.

The name comes from the old trade: a millwright is the person who builds and maintains the machinery that keeps everything running. That's the idea here — dependable tools, built with care rather than chasing trends.

Trellis

Available on Android · iOS coming soon

A task, calendar, and weather app with a garden that grows as you get things done.

How Trellis uses Google Calendar: If you connect it, Trellis lists your subscribed calendars and displays their events alongside your tasks. You can create, edit, or delete events on calendars your Google account can edit. Calendar data travels directly between your device and Google over HTTPS; it does not pass through Trellis's server. Optional weather, feedback, purchase, and Drive backup data are described in the privacy policy.
